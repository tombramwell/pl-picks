Official Game Update

Afternoon,

Thanks for taking part in Premiership Picks.

This is just a quick update to point out a couple of upgrades that should make playing the game even better moving forward.

⚡ Live Match Updates

There are now live updates on the gameweek pages, displaying the current score of the match and the goalscorers as they happen.

📋 Teamsheets & Lineups

If you're one of those who likes to wait until the teams have been confirmed before selecting your player, that data now feeds directly into the app.

When the lineups have been confirmed, you'll see a Teamsheets Out indicator next to the fixture. When you click the dropdown to select your player, their status will be clearly indicated:

✅ = In the starting XI
🪑 = On the bench
❌ = Not selected

⚽ Goalscoring Form

You'll also now see a player's 'goalscoring form' listed alongside their name in the dropdown, letting you know exactly how many goals they've scored so far this season.

Thanks again for playing. Feel free to share the link with anyone you think might want to get involved, and if you have any ideas for features or upgrades, please do get in touch.

Cheers,
Tom
